# Related Landscape

This project should reuse mature standards rather than inventing every primitive. The proposed contribution is an integration layer at the intersection of AI telemetry, workplace knowledge, portable capability, evidence custody, due process, and independent trust.

| Existing work | Reusable contribution | Proposed remaining question |
|---|---|---|
| [Data Transfer Initiative AI portability](https://dtinit.org/ai) and [AI Conversation History schema](https://schemas.pub/schemas/24) | AI-conversation export, service portability, and trust-registry thinking | How should mixed employer and worker knowledge, safe capability abstraction, and employment disputes be handled? |
| [ApertoMemory Internet-Draft](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-ferro-apertomemory/) | Encrypted portable memory objects, signed provenance, custody, trust derivation, and test vectors | How should rights be allocated when workplace evidence is protected or mixed? |
| [W3C Verifiable Credentials 2.0](https://www.w3.org/TR/vc-data-model/) | Issuer, holder, and verifier roles; signed claims; status; terms; privacy | How should confidential evidence custody and workplace dispute procedure work? |
| [1EdTech Open Badges 3.0](https://www.1edtech.org/standards/open-badges) | Portable skill credentials, self-assertions, endorsements, wallets, and certification | How should capability derived from confidential work traces be represented without credential laundering? |
| [W3C PROV](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-overview/) and [OpenLineage](https://github.com/OpenLineage/OpenLineage) | Provenance and lineage for entities, activities, agents, runs, jobs, and datasets | Who decides rights, portability, trust, and remedy? |
| [OpenTelemetry semantic conventions](https://opentelemetry.io/docs/concepts/semantic-conventions/) and [OpenInference](https://github.com/Arize-ai/openinference) | Cross-vendor semantics for traces and AI operations | How should observed work become governed knowledge outcomes? |
| [EU Platform Work Directive 2024/2831](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=OJ:L_202402831) | Legal precedent for worker-data access, explanation, review, representation, and remedy | How can comparable safeguards be expressed across industries and jurisdictions? |
| [MyData principles](https://www.mydata.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/Understanding-MyData-Operators-2022-1.pdf) | Human-centric control, portability, transparency, accountability, and interoperability | How should mixed rights and protected work product be handled inside employment? |

The claim that a gap exists is a hypothesis to test, not a conclusion. Please submit missing projects, conflicting standards, legal precedents, or evidence that the proposed layer is unnecessary through the [related-work issue form](https://github.com/yimoburu/worker-knowledge-portability/issues/new/choose).
