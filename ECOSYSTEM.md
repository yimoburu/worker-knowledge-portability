# Ecosystem

The proposal is an ecosystem, not a single tool.

## Layer 1: Charter

The charter defines worker capability agency, employer knowledge protection, claim/evidence separation, review, appeal, and remedy. It is the minimum floor against which every later component must be tested.

## Layer 2: Open protocol

A future protocol could define:

- Governed capability-claim schema
- Claim-level provenance
- Rights, license, and trust status
- Safe-abstraction contract
- Portability lifecycle
- Review, dispute, and decision records

Protocol work should begin only after the charter survives multi-stakeholder critique.

## Layer 3: Conforming implementations

Possible implementations include worker wallets, employer evidence vaults, boundary engines, platform adapters, and offboarding or review systems. They would be replaceable implementations of shared rules, not one mandatory product.

## Layer 4: Trust ecosystem

Trust requires conformance tests, independent certification, policy profiles, collective representation, neutral review, and an anonymized precedent registry. Technical interoperability without legitimate governance would not solve the problem.

## Governed decision

Every decision produces one of four outcomes:

- Employer-retained
- Shared or licensed
- Worker-portable
- Blocked or disputed

The capability claim and its supporting evidence carry separate provenance, custody, rights, trust, and review status.
