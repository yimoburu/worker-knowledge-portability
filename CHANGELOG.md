# Change log

## Unreleased terminology-contract correction

- Added an explicit memory-to-claim boundary: AI memory records, conversation
  exports, agent-state packages, learned prompts, graph facts, and
  model-derived summaries do not become worker-portable claims merely because
  they are technically exportable or associated with a worker.
- Expanded the implementation adapter category to cover AI memory and agent
  runtimes spanning personal and organizational systems, without selecting a
  mandatory product or changing the ecosystem structure.
- Distinguished receiving organizations from relying parties and aligned the
  Chinese terms on `接收组织` and `依赖方`.
- Replaced the overloaded generic attestation label with the project-specific
  `claim attestation` / `可追溯的能力声明佐证`.
- Reserved `conformity assessment` / `合格评定` and `certification` / `认证`
  for future formal schemes or programs; current ecosystem components use
  independent assessment against published requirements.
- Standardized `disclosure-minimizing abstraction` and expanded the Chinese
  controlled vocabulary to cover the complete English terminology layer.
- Kept the ecosystem architecture, no-implementation boundary, and four
  governed dispositions unchanged.
